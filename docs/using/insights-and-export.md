# Insights, import and export

## Insights

The **Insights** tab covers everything you can see, across all your ledgers:

- **You spent** and **You paid**, with the number of expenses and ledgers.
- **Where it went**: spending by category. Tap a category to see its expenses.
- **Month by month**: spending over time.
- **Most spent with**: the people you share the most expenses with.

Pick a **Date range** at the top: **All time**, **Last 12 months**, **Last 30 days** or **Custom**.

Each ledger also has its own **Insights** section: the ledger's total, spending by category and month, and **By member**, which shows what each person paid against their share.

## Activity

Each ledger's **Activity** section is a feed of everything that happened: expenses added, edited or deleted, settlements, written-off debts, comments, and people joining or leaving. Use **Filter** to narrow it by **Person** or **Type**. The **Ledgers** tab shows recent activity from all your ledgers.

## Import

Bring a history across from Splitwise or Splid.

1. In the ledger options menu (**⋯**), choose **Import**.
2. Pick a **Splid (.xls)** or **Splitwise (.csv)** export. tally shows how many expenses it found and how many rows it skipped.
3. Under **Match each name to a member**, match every name in the file to a member of this ledger. The instance owner can also **Create guest** for a name with no match.
4. Tap **Import**. Nothing is written until this step.

Archived ledgers do not accept imports.

## Export

Your data is yours. Exports are plain files that do not depend on this instance staying up.

From a ledger's **Export** option, or **Export or print** in **Settle**:

| Export                     | What it contains                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Expenses (CSV)**         | Every expense in the ledger, one row each, with who paid and each share.               |
| **Settle-up (CSV)**        | The transfer plan and each member's net position.                                      |
| **Settle-up (Excel)**      | The same settle-up sheets, in one Excel workbook.                                      |
| **Printable report (PDF)** | The settle-up on one page, with every expense behind it. Print it or save it as a PDF. |

To take everything at once, open the **You** tab and tap **Export everything**. It produces one CSV of every ledger you can see.

Exports need a connection.

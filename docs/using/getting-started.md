# Getting started

tally is invite-only. There is no public sign-up, so you start from a link someone sends you.

## Join with an invite

There are two kinds of invite link. Both work once and expire after 48 hours.

- **An invite to tally**, from the instance owner. It creates an account without putting you in any ledger. You get a full account: you can create ledgers and invite people.
- **An invite to a ledger**, from any full member of that ledger. It creates your account and puts you straight into that ledger. You get a restricted account: you can use the ledgers you are in, but you cannot create ledgers or invites. The owner can promote you later.

Open the link, pick a display name, then choose how you sign in:

- **Create passkey**: no password and no email. Your phone or laptop unlocks tally with the face, fingerprint or PIN it already uses.
- **Use an email and password instead**: at least 10 characters. Useful inside an in-app browser, where passkeys often fail.

You can add the other method later. Having both means a lost device is not a locked account.

If the link opened inside WhatsApp, Instagram or another app, tap the **…** menu and open it in Safari or Chrome first. Those built-in browsers usually cannot create passkeys. See the [FAQ](../FAQ.md#passkey-sign-in-fails-inside-whatsapp-instagram-or-messenger).

## Find your way around

The tab bar has four tabs:

- **Ledgers**: your ledgers, starred ones first, with what you are owed or owe overall and recent activity.
- **Balances**: what you and each person owe overall, across every ledger you share.
- **Insights**: where your money went, by category, month and person.
- **You**: your profile, sign-in methods, theme, notifications and exports.

Inside a ledger, the sections are **Expenses**, **Settle**, **Insights**, **Activity** and **Recurring expenses**. Every other ledger action is in the ledger options menu (**⋯**).

## Set up your profile

Open the **You** tab.

- **Display name**: how you appear to everyone you share a ledger with.
- **UPI VPA**: your UPI address, like `name@bank`. With it, people can pay you back in one tap. Only people you share a ledger with can see it.
- **Theme**: System, Paper, Light, or one of the dark themes. It applies to this device only.

## Passkeys and passwords

Both work on the same account. Manage them in the **Sign-in and security** section of the **You** tab.

- **Add passkey** registers this device. To sign in on another device, choose "use a phone or tablet" when your browser asks.
- **Set a password** needs an email, which is set once along with the password.
- **Revoke** removes a passkey. tally will not let you revoke your only passkey without another way in.
- **Sign out** ends this session on this device only.

## Install it on your phone

tally is a web app you can install.

1. Open tally in your browser and do one thing, such as saving an expense. An **Install** prompt appears after that. It never appears on the first visit.
2. On iPhone or iPad there is no prompt. In Safari, open the share menu and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
3. When a new version is ready, tally shows **Reload**. It never reloads by itself, so a half-filled form is safe.

## Notifications

On the **You** tab, tap **Enable notifications**. tally sends two kinds only: someone settles with you, and a settle reminder from someone you owe. On iPhone and iPad, install tally to the Home Screen first; web push only works from an installed app.

Push notifications have not yet been tested on a real phone. If one does not arrive, see the [FAQ](../FAQ.md#are-push-notifications-working).

## Offline

tally needs a connection to show or change money. Offline, it hides every amount and refuses to save, so you never see a stale balance or lose an expense to a hidden queue. Try again once you are back online.

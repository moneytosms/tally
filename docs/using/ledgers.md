# Ledgers and people

A ledger is one shared set of expenses with its own members. Balances never mix between ledgers, except on the **Balances** tab, which adds them up per person.

## Create a ledger

Tap **New ledger** and pick a kind:

- **New trip** has an end date. Add an optional budget and tally shows how much is left to spend per day until the trip ends.
- **New group** runs indefinitely: a flatshare, a regular crowd.
- **New one-on-one** is for costs shared with one other person.

Give it a name. Optionally pick a colour and one emoji for its tile. Under **Add the same people as**, you can copy the members of an existing ledger.

Restricted accounts cannot create ledgers.

## Add people

Open the ledger options menu (**⋯**) and choose **Members**.

- **Add someone with an account** adds anyone already on this instance. They join as themselves.
- **Add a guest** adds a person who never signs in. A guest splits and pays like anyone else, and someone else records their settlements. Only the instance owner can add guests.
- **Invite link** creates a single-use link for one new person (see below).

### Invite links

Invite links are off for each ledger unless someone turns them on.

1. In the ledger options menu, turn on **Allow invite links**.
2. Tap **Invite someone**, then **Copy link**.
3. Send it to one person over a private channel. It works once and expires after 48 hours.

Anyone holding the link joins as themselves, so treat it like a key. Turning **Allow invite links** off immediately stops every link already handed out. The person who joins gets a restricted account.

### Guests who join later

If a guest later gets an account, open **Members**, tap **Merge into...** on the guest and pick their real member. Every expense the guest paid or shared moves across, and the guest is retired. This cannot be undone.

## Leave or remove someone

- **Leave this ledger** works once your net position is zero. Settle up first, or have someone write off what is outstanding.
- **Remove** (instance owner only) has the same rule for the person being removed.

Past expenses stay exactly where they are in both cases.

## Star, edit and duplicate

- **Star** pins a ledger to the top of the **Ledgers** tab.
- **Edit details** changes the name, colour, emoji, end date and budget. A budget only means something alongside an end date.
- **Duplicate** creates a new ledger with the same members, and no expenses.

## Archive, reopen and delete

- **Archive** closes a finished ledger. It needs every net position at zero. An archived ledger is read-only: no new expenses, settlements or imports, and its recurring expenses stop.
- **Reopen** brings an archived ledger back.
- **Delete** is only available to whoever created the ledger. Nothing is hard-deleted; the ledger is hidden.

Footnotes

  1. Bring your history: import from Splitwise or Splid, and export any ledger to CSV.
  2. Sign in with a passkey or a password. Accounts are by invite only.
  3. It installs as a PWA and runs on one Cloudflare Worker with D1, at $0 a month.
  4. Built for about 20 people per instance, one group of friends.
